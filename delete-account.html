How to delete your LocalPlay account

Option 1: Delete it in the app (recommended)

  1. Open LocalPlay and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete permanently.

This permanently deletes your profile, your registrations, and any tournaments you organised. It cannot be undone.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, email social28social@gmail.com from the address or mobile number you registered with, with the subject "Delete my LocalPlay account". We will delete your account and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

Your profile details, your reports and blocks, your notification token, and the tournaments you organised.

What may remain

Photos you uploaded may remain in storage for a short time. Purchase records kept by Google Play are governed by Google's own policies.
