Delete your TournamentFx account

Last updated: [DATE]

Delete in the app

  1. Open TournamentFx and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then scroll down to Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

If you can't open the app

Email social28social@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Include your mobile number and say you want your account deleted. We'll confirm once it's done. We may ask you to verify that the request came from you.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, city, state, email, mobile number and profile photo.
  • Your registrations, tournament follows, notification tokens and blocks.
  • Tournaments you organised, and their updates and photos.
  • Ads you posted, and their photos.

What may be kept

  • Reports you sent or received may be kept briefly for safety and legal reasons.
  • Database backups are kept for [30] days, then deleted. Backups are private and are used only to restore the service.
