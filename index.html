LocalPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

LocalPlay ("we", "the app") helps local players find and share sports tournaments. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, city, state, and either your mobile number or your email address.
  • Profile photo: an image you choose to upload.
  • Tournament details: titles, dates, venues, contact information and banner photos you post as an organiser.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you send about tournaments or players, and players you block.
  • Notification token: a device identifier that lets us send you alerts for new tournaments in your state, if you turn alerts on.
  • Purchases: when you pay to spotlight a tournament, payment is handled by Google Play. We receive a confirmation to verify the purchase and extend the spotlight. We do not receive your card details.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, show tournaments, send state alerts you have chosen, verify purchases, and keep the app safe. Reports are reviewed by our admins.

Who we share it with

Your data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database, login and photo storage), Firebase (push notifications), and Google Play (payments). Other players can see your name, city and state, and tournament information you publish. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile and the tournaments you organised are removed.

Your choices

  • Turn state alerts on or off in the app.
  • Edit your name, city, state and photo in My profile.
  • Delete your account. See how to delete your account.
