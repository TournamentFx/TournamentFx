TournamentFx Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information the TournamentFx app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies when you use the app. By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

1. Who we are

TournamentFx is operated by TournamentFx. For any privacy question, email social28social@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account details. Your name, email address, mobile number, city and state. Your password is stored in encrypted form by our sign-in service. We never see it in plain text.

Profile photo. Optional. You choose the photo from your phone.

Tournaments you host. Title, sport, dates, venue, entry fee, prizes, description, banner photo, organiser name, and any contact numbers you enter.

Your activity in the app. Tournaments you follow, tournaments you register for, updates you post as an organiser, reports you send, and players you block.

Bats and gear ads. Title, description, price, contact number, city, state, and an optional photo.

Notification token. A device token that lets us send you alerts.

Activity counts. We record which tournaments you open, counted once per person, and when the app was last open. Admins see only totals, such as how many people are online and how many people viewed each tournament. They can't see who viewed what.

Diagnostics and usage. Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect app usage events, crash reports, and device information such as model, operating system version and app version. Firebase may also collect an advertising identifier, depending on your phone settings. We don't use it to show you advertising.

What we don't collect. The app doesn't read your precise location and doesn't use GPS. It can access photos only when you choose one to upload.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep it secure.
  • To show tournaments and ads, and to let you host, follow and post.
  • To send alerts: new tournaments in your state (if you have alerts on), and updates from organisers you follow.
  • To approve tournaments, handle reports and blocks, and remove content that breaks our rules.
  • To show admins how many people are online and how many viewed each tournament.
  • To find and fix crashes, and to keep the app working.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to requests from authorities.

4. Who can see your information

  • Anyone, including people who haven't signed in: the details of approved tournaments, including the organiser's name and any contact numbers the organiser entered.
  • Signed-in players: bats and gear ads, including the seller's contact number; your name, city, state and photo; and, if the players directory is switched on, your name, city and state in that directory.
  • Organisers: whether you follow their tournament, and your registration.
  • Admins: your account details, reports, and activity totals. Admins can remove tournaments, ads and accounts that break our rules.
  • Service providers who process data for us:
    • Supabase, which hosts our database, file storage and sign-in service.
    • Google Firebase, which delivers notifications and provides analytics and crash reports.
    • Brevo, which sends emails such as password-reset codes.
    • Google Play, only if we offer paid features in future.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

5. Your mobile number

We use your mobile number to sign you in. It isn't shown publicly unless you type it into a tournament or an ad you post. Think before you publish your number.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, mobile number, registrations, follows, notification tokens, profile photos, and ad photos. Tournaments you organised and ads you posted are removed too. Backups of the database are kept for [30] days, then deleted. Records of failed sign-in attempts are kept for a short time, under an hour, to stop people guessing passwords.

7. Your choices

  • Change your name, city, state, photo, email and password in your profile.
  • Turn alerts on or off, and follow or unfollow any tournament.
  • Block a player you don't want to hear from.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), or follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Notifications are controlled by your phone's settings too.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent. To make a request or a complaint, email social28social@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

TournamentFx is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Our database uses access rules so each person can only read and change their own private information. Admin tools and backups are restricted to admin accounts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the date at the top, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
